Summary

This app helps Urdu speakers learn Pashto. It does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not sell personal information.

Data we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts through this app, and we do not require you to sign in to use it.

Online search and content updates

Most content works offline. If you use the in-app online search, your search words are sent to the search service used for that result (Wiktionary definitions, or DuckDuckGo web search) so that results can be returned. Those services have their own privacy policies. Content updates are downloaded from this website; no account or personal information is sent with an update check.

Data stored on your device

App content and any app settings are stored on your own device. We do not receive a copy of that local data.

Children

The app is a general language-learning app. It is not directed at collecting personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
